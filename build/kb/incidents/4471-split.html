#4471 is two problems merged as one. One cause owns the ticket. The other was merged into it by mistake and had no ticket of its own — the desk only sees that when it reads each report's cause, not its subject.

Both are problems this ticket holds; both now have their own ticket in this repo.

parser — 379 reports

The shared parser reads every date month first. Every tenant on file writes dates day first, so their dates come out flipped: a “7 April” reminder goes out on 4 July.

  • All 11 tenants, all three channels: app 204 · email 150 · form 25. The 25 form reports run booking-v2, whose DD/MM/YYYY hint agrees with how these tenants read — the hint does not flip them; the parser does.
  • Seen 2026-04-01 → 2026-10-04, still arriving when the export was cut.
  • Intake tagged 356 of these parser and 23 hint, guessed from the reporter’s browser locale. The tag is a guess; the cause depends on the tenant, which intake never looks up.
  • Five reports (R-41202, R-41209, R-41236, R-41344, R-41620) read as a one-day move — a timezone edge, like a client in Lisbon getting Tuesday while Toronto gets Monday. The desk read them differently but files them under parser by tenant, counted here and footnoted, per desk-notes.
  • Ticket: #1 · parser — 379 reports

hint — 33 reports

booking-v3 prints the hint MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. The three tenants running it — alder.physio (de-DE), copper.studio (es-ES), pale.harbour (pt-PT) — all write dates day first, so they type what the hint shows, month first, and the form reads their input day first: booked 6 August, invite out on 8 June.

  • All 33 reports came through the form channel. The same three tenants’ 63 app and email reports are the parser problem, not this one — the hint only bites where it is printed.
  • Seen 2026-04-17 → 2026-09-22 (alder.physio 12 · copper.studio 11 · pale.harbour 10).
  • Intake tagged 15 of these parser. Again the browser locale, not the tenant, decided.
  • This is a different bug from parser: here the tenant’s input matches the form’s own hint, and the form disagrees with itself.
  • Ticket: #2 · hint — 33 reports

Why they merged

Decision 0001 made a duplicate a matching subject line. Both problems write the same subjects — “Invite came on the wrong day”, “Month and day swapped?” — so subject matching could not tell them apart, and nobody read the cause. The triage explorer runs the desk’s cause test on exactly those pairs: same subject, different causes, keep apart.

Since April

The desk kept working through all of it: reports arrived every week from 2026-03-30 until the export was cut on 2026-10-04, and were merged into #4471 as they came.

379 + 33 = 412: every unique report in the export is covered by exactly one of the two tickets.

Where #4471 ended

On 5 October the desk closed #4471 with an engineering update:

  • parser — FIXED: fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales. The parser now reads dates the way EU locales write them. The 379 reports above are why the fix exists.
  • hint — WON’T FIX: the booking-v3 form was retired. The 33 reports above end with the product, not with a code fix — the desk closed them anyway, with the counts on record.

Every one of the 412 reports is now accounted for: fixed, or retired with the form that caused it.
